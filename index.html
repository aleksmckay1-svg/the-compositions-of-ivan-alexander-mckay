The Compositions of Ivan-Alexander McKay

Ivan-Alexander 'Aleks' McKay (born 12 April 1998) is a Scottish composer. The son of a Scottish father and a Bulgarian mother, he studied at the University of Glasgow and there obtained an MRes for musical composition. He has worked as a tutor.

I've played several organ works by Aleks, all of which have been fresh and fascinating. One of the striking features of his writing is the ability to create vital soundscapes from small figures. His use of harmony, too, is emotive, dramatic, and memorable. His music is lyrical, rewarding to play, practical, and appreciated by audience and player alike.

Kevin Bowyer

I had the pleasure of supervising Aleks's portfolio for his MRes as well as during his fourth year of the MA. During this time, I came to understand the deeply thoughtful and genuinely unique way that Aleks composes. His MRes portfolio is testament to his productivity and high creative energy. He demonstrated a particular strength in writing for voices and for piano, often very virtuosically, and always knowledgeably. He also has a meticulous eye for detail and presentation.

Jane Stanley

[Aleks goes] about [his] work with a dedicated single-mindedness that is rare and admirable.

Drew Hammond
